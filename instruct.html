CGPA Calculator (AI & DS - 2022 Regulation)

A simple and effective web tool designed specifically for Artificial Intelligence and Data Science students under the 2022 regulation to track academic performance.

Live Demo: Access the calculator directly at sri-kanth-j.github.io/gpa

Step-by-Step User Instructions

  1. Go to the Calculator: Open sri-kanth-j.github.io/gpa on your browser.
  2. Select Semesters: Choose the number of semesters you want to calculate (Semesters 1 through 6). Click Generate Forms to load your subjects.
  3. Select Your Grades: The required subject codes, titles, and credit values will display automatically. Choose your earned grade (e.g., O, A+, A, B+) for each course from the drop-down menu.
  4. Calculate GPA/CGPA: Click the Calculate CGPA button at the bottom to compute your semester GPA and overall cumulative performance.
  5. Start Over using the Reset Button: If you want to erase all chosen grades and start fresh from the beginning, click the Clear button. This wipes away your current inputs across all semesters so you can re-enter fresh grades.

Qualitative Grade Interpretation

Your calculated score maps to performance indicators as outlined below:

GPA Range Qualitative Grade
9.0 and above Excellent
8.0 – 8.9 Very Good
7.0 – 7.9 Good
6.0 – 6.9 Average
Below 6.0 Below Average / Fail (as per institution rules)

Features & System Notes

  • Curriculum Auto-Loading: Pre-populates exact subject list and credit mapping for the AI & DS curriculum (2022 Regulation).
  • Grade Preservation: Keeps your selected grades intact even if you change the total semester count or switch options while entering marks.
  • High Accuracy: Calculation logic is verified directly against official institutional mark sheets.
Note & Limitations: Subject definitions are currently mapped for Semesters 1 through 6. Selecting an option outside this range automatically resets the page.
